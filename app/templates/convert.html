Media Converter

Resize any image, GIF, or video to an exact Instagram/Pinterest canvas size - not tied to a CJ product, just a general resizer for whatever you've got.

File (image, GIF, or video) Target size Fit style (images only)

"Fit" places the whole photo on a blurred letterbox background when its shape doesn't match the target - good for feed posts where nothing should get cropped out. "Crop to fill" instead fills the entire frame with sharp pixels and crops off the excess - better for small placements like a storefront category icon, where a blurred border can make the whole thumbnail read as blurry. GIFs and videos always scale + center-crop to fill the frame (no letterbox) regardless of this setting - videos come back as .mp4, GIFs stay .gif. Large videos can take a little while to process.
